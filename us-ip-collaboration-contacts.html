Four items to prepare now

  1. Dining introduction: your team, restaurant partner, operating history, customer profile and each party’s responsibilities.
  2. Dining proposal prototype: venue photos, capacity, sample menu direction, décor zones, run length, budget range and marketing plan.
  3. Exhibition introduction: production capability, venue access and exhibition delivery partners. Do not imply these are secured unless they are.
  4. Exhibition proposal template: concept, visitor journey, footprint, budget, schedule and the rights you need.

Lead with the experience-rights brief

  1. Pick one primary IP and one alternative for each contact.

  4. Ask about category authority, local exclusivity, minimum commercial requirements and approval timing.
  5. Develop full creative assets after the contact confirms a plausible rights route.
